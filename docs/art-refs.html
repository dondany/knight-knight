Sprite rules

What the game does with a sprite once it is in characters.PNG. These come from the code, so a sprite that follows them drops in without touching anything else.

The cell

  • One unit is one 16×16 cell. The sheet is five cells wide, with one row per army.
  • Every army row is full, so new units need new rows. Only the mercenary row has free cells. A unit's cell is set in its def(...) line, so rows can go in any order.
  • A sprite may stick out of its cell by a pixel if it has to; the Samurai and the Sellsword already do. That needs a one-off origin override in the unit's definition.

What the game adds

  • A 1-pixel dark outline round the whole silhouette. Do not draw an outer outline yourself.
  • The background is removed by colour: whatever the top-left pixel of the sheet is, today #151d28. That one palette colour cannot be used inside a sprite.
  • The white hit flash and the grey-green Mummy version are generated. The Mummy is recoloured by brightness alone, so a sprite needs light and dark areas to stay readable as one.

Pose

  • Units face right. The enemy side is the same sprite mirrored, so anything that reads left-to-right will flip.
  • The idle animation shifts rows 0–9 down one pixel on every other beat and leaves rows 10–15 still. Row 9 is hidden while that happens.
  • So: feet, hooves and wheels go in rows 10–15, and no detail that matters goes on row 9.
  • House style in the current sheet: weapon upright along the right edge, two-pixel legs, a head about four pixels wide.

The palette

The sheet is drawn in the 46 colours of the Apollo palette, and the game's backgrounds and interface use the same set. The swatches under each unit are picked from it.
